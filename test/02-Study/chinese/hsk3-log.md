Running log of daily Chinese practice. See [[hsk-roadmap]] for the overall HSK1→HSK3 plan and phases.

## 2026-09-29 — Day 1, Phase 0 (Pinyin & Tones foundation)

Absolute beginner start — no characters or grammar yet, just pinyin sounds and tones.

**Tones:** learn to hear and produce the 4 tones + neutral tone on the syllable *ma*: mā (1st, high flat) / má (2nd, rising) / mǎ (3rd, dip) / mà (4th, falling) / ma (neutral). Use a tone-trainer to drill tone pairs — see [[hsk-roadmap]] resources.

**Pinyin initials/finals:** read through the full pinyin chart aloud once, just to see the shape of the system — no need to memorize yet.

**Survival words (pronunciation only, no characters required today):**
| Pinyin | Meaning |
|---|---|
| nǐ hǎo | hello |
| xièxie | thank you |
| bú kèqi | you're welcome |
| zàijiàn | goodbye |
| duìbuqǐ | sorry / excuse me |
| shì | to be / yes |

| wǒ | I / me |
| nǐ | you |
| xièxie nǐ | thank you (to you) |

**Exit check for today:** can say each survival word aloud with roughly the right tone shape, and can tell the 4 tones apart by ear.
