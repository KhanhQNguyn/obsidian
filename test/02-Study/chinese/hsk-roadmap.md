# Chinese — HSK1 to HSK3 Roadmap

Starting point: zero prior Chinese knowledge. Goal: conversational HSK3. Daily entries tracked in [[hsk3-log]].

## Reality check on timeline

Estimates vary a lot by source and by how much daily time is put in:

- HSK1 (~150 words, old-syllabus list still widely used for absolute beginners): roughly 30–150 study hours depending on pace, commonly cited as 1–3 months at 20–45 min/day.
- HSK2 (~300 words cumulative): roughly 300 hours total from zero, often ~10 months at 1 hour/day — but far less if HSK1 is already solid, since HSK2 mostly builds on it.
- HSK1→HSK3 combined: most adult learners land HSK3 in **4–6 months** at a moderate pace (300–500 total hours), assuming 30–45 min/day, 5–6 days/week.

Treat these as ranges, not deadlines — actual pace depends on consistency and whether characters are learned alongside pinyin (slower but sturdier) or pinyin-first (faster early progress, characters added after).

## Phases

### Phase 0 — Pinyin & Tones foundation (~1–2 weeks)
Before any vocab: pinyin initials/finals and the 4 tones (+ neutral tone). This is the single highest-leverage investment — skipping it makes everything after harder to pronounce and to hear.
- Daily: tone pair drills, initial/final chart read-aloud, tone-trainer app reps.
- Exit condition: can read any pinyin syllable aloud with correct tone without hesitating.

### Phase 1 — HSK1 (~4–8 weeks)
~150 core words, basic sentence patterns (是, 有, 在, simple questions with 吗/呢, numbers, dates, basic time expressions).
- Daily: 5–10 new words with example sentences, 1 grammar pattern every few days, short listening clips, spaced repetition review of everything already learned.
- Exit condition: can introduce yourself, ask/answer simple questions, handle basic transactions (ordering, asking prices, directions) in slow speech.

### Phase 2 — HSK2 (~6–10 weeks)
~150 additional words (300 cumulative), more connectors (因为/所以, 虽然/但是), comparisons (比), time/aspect markers (了, 过, 在 + verb).
- Daily: new vocab batch + grammar pattern, short dialogues, start reading very simple graded text.
- Exit condition: can hold a slow basic conversation about daily life, follow simple graded audio/video.

### Phase 3 — HSK3 (~8–14 weeks)
~300 additional words (600 cumulative), more complex grammar (把-construction, 是...的, complements of degree/result, more connectors), longer listening/reading passages.
- Daily: vocab + grammar as before, but push toward producing longer sentences and short paragraphs, more listening at natural-ish speed.
- Exit condition: can discuss familiar topics (work, study, hobbies, travel) with some complexity, read short essays/notices.

## Daily practice shape (once past Phase 0)
A workable daily block, adaptable to whatever time is available:
1. Review due spaced-repetition cards (yesterday's + older vocab)
2. Learn 5–10 new words in context (not isolated)
3. One grammar point or sentence pattern, with 2–3 example sentences self-produced
4. 5–15 min listening or reading at current level
5. Log what was covered in [[hsk3-log]]
