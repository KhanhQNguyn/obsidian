# Lessons Learned

2 hackathons failed, and lessons learned
- don't work with your friends
- slides should be consulted
- don't stick to coding the actual runnable application, mockups are HIGHLY RECOMMENDED, the judges only need to know how it works (ideally)
- Use AI videos for editing and demoing will save time.
- don't work for the disabled
2 final inverview fails and lessons learned
- don't talk too much if the interviewer is not interested. Talk slow, talk enough, straight to the point, and do not introduce new knowledge while talking so that I won't get questioned many times about the things I ACCIDENTALLY SAY. Keep everything simple, don't talk much, they will continue to ask if they are interested. If do this, it will not only helps me to avoid trick questions, but also train my mind to be short about things.
- ==Should find out about company's domain knowledge very carefully, what are the main jobs that they hire, what are their responsibilities
- Care about how the work are integrated with AI, and its working life cycle
- Always be listening actively to any guidance given and extend it even further (always do better than what's told)
