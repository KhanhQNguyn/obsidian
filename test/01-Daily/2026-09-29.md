**Meeting prep, learn-along (minimal words, one idea at a time):**

- Loan = bank gives money now, you pay back in pieces later.
- Underwriting = the check bank does before saying yes. Right now, a person does this check by hand. Hand-check = slow.
- Bureau (CIC) = a record book of who borrowed before, who paid back. Bank reads this book to judge risk.
- Empty book = bank says no. Not because you're bad. Because bank has no record on you.
- Gig workers, small sellers: they earn real money, through apps/wallets/online sales. But bureau book stays empty for them. So bank keeps saying no to good payers.
- Alt-data = proof of good behavior that isn't the bureau book. App income, wallet top-ups, on-time bills, steady online sales. This data exists already. Bank just isn't reading it.
- STP = a process that runs by itself, no human. Today only safest customers get this. Goal: give STP to more people, safely.
- eKYC = check your ID with camera + database, not paper. Fast, still safe.
- Explainable = bank must say *why* it said no, not just say no. Simple models can explain themselves. Black-box AI can't. Bank wants explainable, even if less flashy.
- Point-of-purchase loan ≠ normal loan. Normal loan: branch, wait days, fine. This loan: you're at checkout now, want yes/no in minutes, or you walk away.
- The numbers: every check costs 380,000 VND flat, big loan or small. Small loan (1.6tr) can lose money just from the check. And ~44% of applicants (gig, first-timer, small seller) get stuck this way — that's not a small side-case, that's near half.
- Bottom line: one root cause (manual, bureau-only check), not three separate problems. Slow + costly + blind-to-good-payers are just three symptoms of that one cause.
